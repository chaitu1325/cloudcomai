Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information CloudComAI collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide, such as username, email, and optional phone number.
  • Device information used for security, such as device verification and login alerts.
  • Message metadata needed for delivery (message content itself is end-to-end encrypted).

How we use information

We use your information to operate CloudComAI, keep your account secure, provide customer support, and improve our AI features such as spam detection and translation.

Your choices

  • Enable or disable locked chats, screenshot alerts, and the approval system at any time.
  • Request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account.
  • Control notification and privacy settings from within the app.

Data retention

We keep account data only as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@cloudcomai.com.
